A lane returned no work when the bounded ready ring contained only sibling-owned tasks, although unadmitted files still contained its tasks. The lane then sent DirectoryEnd and exited; the remaining lane eventually also exhausted its own visible work. This is premature EOF, not a physical link failure.

Add src/core/io/dynamic_file_queue.cpp to affected implementation files. Add deterministic bounded-ring regression and cancellation/timeout tests before fixing. Preserve bounded capacity and lane ownership; wait for admission progress while unadmitted work exists, wake on cancellation/stop/owner change, and preserve bounded timeout. Do not expand the ring or silently drop affinity.
